Research & Writing

📄
ICER '17 · ACM · Pages 56–64
Taking Advantage of Scale by Analyzing Frequent Constructed-Response, Code Tracing Wrong Answers
Kristin Stephens-Martinez, An Ju, Krishna Parashar, Regina Ongowarsito, Nikunj Jain, Sreesha Venkat, Armando Fox

Explores how wrong answers at scale — from large CS courses — can be systematically analyzed to reveal common student misconceptions in code tracing. By clustering frequent incorrect constructed responses, instructors can identify and address gaps in understanding far more efficiently than manual review allows.
